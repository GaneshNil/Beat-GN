# Feature recommendations

Use the BXSM2001 manual as product-level evidence and the repository’s prior DaFit analysis as family-level protocol evidence. Firmware V006213 remains physically untested. [Manufacturer instruction manual](https://www.manualslib.com/manual/4141784/Beatxp-Marv-Neo-Bxsm2001.html) · [Historical protocol evidence](PROTOCOL_EVIDENCE.md)

| Priority and candidate | Evidence | Scope, complexity and privacy | V006213 test |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Improve device information and diagnostics | **PARTIALLY VERIFIED.** The app has standard Device Information Service reads and metadata-only diagnostics. | Low. Record characteristic properties and subscription results without device addresses or raw data. Android GATT standard reads only. | Compare model and firmware with watch About and a read-only GATT inventory. |
| 2. Alarm read-back and slot reconciliation | **PARTIALLY VERIFIED.** The DaFit SDK documents a `0xB9` query. Firmware response format and interpretation remain open. | Medium. Decode a captured reply on FEE3, then compare slot, time, enabled state and repeat days. Keep reconciliation separate from retransmitting the write. Alarm times remain local. | Query before and after create, edit and disable. Verify all five slots and watch-side display. |
| 3. Local health export and more detailed activity reports | **CONFIRMED** inputs at the app layer. Room stores timestamped pulse, SpO₂, BP and complete activity snapshots. | Low to medium. Use Room and Storage Access Framework `ACTION_CREATE_DOCUMENT`. No new BLE requests or broad storage permission. Label locally recorded data and make health-file sharing explicit. Activity reports should use received snapshots, with dates and units verified first. | Export empty, multi-day and DST examples. Compare with Room. Verify activity units and midnight counters on the watch. |
| 4. Historical activity, pulse and sleep sync | **PARTIALLY VERIFIED** at the device-family level for history. The exact operation and clock semantics are **UNKNOWN** on V006213. | High. Capture vendor requests and response continuation on sanctioned device sessions. Design repeat-safe imports and define time source and record identifiers. Timeline data is sensitive. | Make records with the phone disconnected. Reconnect, check against watch/DaFit, repeat sync, and compare after midnight. |
| 5. Battery/power behavior | **PARTIALLY VERIFIED.** Standard battery read and connected-device foreground service are implemented. | Medium. Measure screen-off radio and process behavior before reducing the heartbeat or adopting Android companion presence APIs. No raw telemetry needed for profiling. | Screen-off battery and reconnect tests across Android 11, 12 and 14–16; include one OEM with battery controls. |
| 6. Camera shutter, music and notification mirroring | **UNKNOWN** on V006213 over this app’s BLE path. Camera and music appear in product documentation, with family implementations varying. | Medium to high. Camera uses an explicit foreground capture screen. Music should respect media-session access. Mirroring needs explicit notification access and per-app privacy controls. Require observed wire behavior first. | Isolate each feature in the vendor app and compare watch and phone events. Include permission denial and sensitive notification handling before any mirroring. |
| 7. Stress, settings, coaching, watch faces and calling | **UNKNOWN** for Beat-GN and V006213 transport. Model-level calling uses a separate pairing flow. | Varies. Read-only capability discovery has lower risk than writes. Watch faces and firmware paths need independently verified formats and explicit project scope. | Compare watch menus against the exact firmware. Gather evidence before using vendor-specific writes. |
| Assistant transcripts | **UNSUPPORTED** via the known Beat-GN protocol path. | No transcript delivery source is documented for this implementation. Keep legacy entry provenance visible. | A real watch-to-phone transcript event would be required to reconsider. |
